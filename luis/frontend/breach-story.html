Chapter 5 · Before the model

The data is mostly text

Exploit telemetry is thin: a handful of 0/1 flags and an EPSS score that is near zero for almost everything. The description, the vendor and the release pattern carry most of what can be known.

Exploit telemetry in 2026

Where the signal lives

Best single-feature AUC per feature group.

The leak trap

NVD status and reference counts are filled in after the fact. Alone they find almost nothing.

Not-yet-analyzed CVEs reach KEV at 0.000%, so NVD status removes a quarter of the haystack. That makes it a filter. It doesn't rank. We left it out.
